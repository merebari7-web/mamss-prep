The GateHow access works

  1. The school issues your slip. Numbered, printed, one per student — like MAMSS-4X9K2M-2026. WhatsApp the office above if you don't have one yet.
  2. You activate one device. Your name plus the slip opens the app. The slip is consumed the moment it is accepted — it cannot be reused on that or any other phone.
  3. The ledger keeps it honest. A second phone presenting your slip is refused with the date it was first used. Offline activations are provisional and reconciled the moment a device reconnects; a conflicting claim is revoked and the device is told why.
